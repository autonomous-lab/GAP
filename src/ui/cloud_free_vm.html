04 / Safety

Bounded by design.

Anonymous outbound access is limited to approved package registries. Arbitrary internet access, private networks, scanning and SSH forwarding are blocked. Package traffic is rate- and volume-limited. Capacity attempts that fail do not create a VM.

Good to know.

Do I need to sign up?

No. An account and verified email are required only if you choose to claim the VM.

Does disconnecting stop it?

No. It remains available for the rest of its one-hour build window.

What happens if I do not claim it?

The VM stops after one hour. Its disk is deleted when the 24-hour claim window expires.

Can others see my preview?

Yes, if you share the URL and Basic Auth credentials. No SSH source-IP match is required.
